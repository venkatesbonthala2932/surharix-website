02 / HOW IT WORKS

From your company down to each task on each site — and back up to one simple dashboard.

1

Add your sites

Set up your company and every construction site you are running.

2

Plan the work

Break each site into towers, floors and tasks — with a budget for each.

3

Update daily

Site supervisors record progress, photos, attendance and material from their phone.

4

Surharix calculates

Work done vs remaining, stock left, and money spent vs budget — automatically.

5

You see everything

Every site on one dashboard — spot delays and overspending early.

08 / FAQ
What is Surharix?add

Surharix is construction site management software. It helps builders track work progress, materials, labour, safety and costs across all their sites in one place.

Who is it for?add

Anyone who builds — builders, contractors and developers, big or small — along with their supervisors and site teams.

Can I manage more than one site?add

Yes. Surharix is built for builders running several sites at once, and shows all of them on one dashboard.

My site staff are not very technical. Will they manage?add

Yes. Daily updates take only a few taps on a phone, and our team helps you get everyone started.
